Engineered for Focus & Privacy

Built purely around your media habits with zero friction and zero distractions.

⚙

Local-First Architecture

Everything runs on an isolated SQLite database right on your device. Instant launches, zero cloud lag, and complete offline capability.

☰

Category Grouping

Active tracks are organized by Shows, Movies, Books, Comics, and Manga. Advancing a track bubbles it to the top of its category group.

⇄

2-Step Fluid Gestures

Single-motion quick swipe right to pause, deep pull to delete, and quick swipe left to edit episode/volume progress directly.

✨

Google Sans & M3

15-tier typography scale, dynamic surface elevation containers, and authentic Material 3 rounded navigation components.

🔍

Metadata & Barcode Scanner

Scan book ISBN barcodes with your camera or look up shows and movies with manual fallbacks for everything.

🛡

100% Tested Domain

Pure TypeScript business logic separated completely from UI and SQLite I/O, backed by 304 automated unit tests.
